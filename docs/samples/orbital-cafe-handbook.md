# Orbital Café — Staff Handbook (Sample Document)

This is a fictional handbook for trying out document question answering.

## Opening hours

The café is open Monday to Friday from 07:30 to 18:00, and Saturday from 09:00 to 15:00.
It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. The last hot-food order is taken 30 minutes before closing.

## Allergies and dietary requests

All allergy questions go to the shift lead on duty. For anything the shift lead cannot answer, contact the
kitchen manager, Priya Raman, at kitchen@orbital-cafe.example. Never guess about allergens. The allergen
binder is kept behind the counter next to the card reader and is updated every Monday.

## Loyalty programme

Customers get one stamp per hot drink. Ten stamps earn a free drink of any size. Stamps expire after
twelve months. Staff may not stamp their own cards.

## Cash handling

Tills are counted at opening and closing by two people. Differences above 5 euros are recorded in the
cash log and reported to the general manager, Tomás Ortega, the same day.

## Uniform

Staff wear the navy apron and closed-toe shoes. Name badges are required on the shop floor.
